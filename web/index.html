Carbonated water98.6% OF THE CAN
Triple-filtered, chilled to 3 °C, then carbonated to 3.8 volumes of CO₂ — high enough to sting, low enough to finish.
Citric acidTHE BITE
Sets the pH at 3.2. It is what makes the first mouthful read as sharp rather than sweet.
Natural lime extractCOLD-PRESSED PEEL OIL
Pressed, never distilled, so the top note survives pasteurisation. Zero Lime carries roughly twice the load of Diet Classic.
SucraloseSWEETNESS, NO SUGAR
Around 600 times as sweet as sucrose, which is why 12 mg does the work of two teaspoons and contributes no calories.
Potassium citrateTHE BALANCE
Buffers the acid so the finish is clean instead of sour, and puts 40 mg of potassium back in.
Caffeine32 MG PER CAN
About a third of a filter coffee. Enough to notice on a slow afternoon, not enough to argue with bedtime.

03 · THE CAN

Aluminium goes
round forever

Glass is heavy and plastic degrades every time it is reprocessed. Aluminium does neither — it can be melted and rolled indefinitely without losing anything, which is why the whole can is one material and the ink is water-based.

100%Recyclable, and recyclable again — aluminium loses no quality in reprocessing.
68%Recycled content in every can we fill this year, up from 51% in 2023.
60Days for a kerbside can to be melted, rolled, filled and back on a shelf.
0.9 kgCO₂e per 24-pack, shipped. Recycled aluminium takes 5% of the energy of new.
